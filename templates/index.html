Template G — the new one

Chapters

The scroll you liked, kept exactly. What changed is that every chapter now owns a muted jewel tone — and the colour is doing a job, not decorating: as you scroll the page changes hue, and the hue tells you which chapter you are in.

The seven chapters

01 Programmes 02 Method 03 Proof 04 Trainers 05 The app 06 Clients 07 Cities

What it does when you touch it

  • Programme matcher — pick the room, then the team; the list narrows live, and the enquiry button carries your choice into the contact form
  • Counters that count up the first time the numbers scroll into view
  • Review spotlight — one at a time, moved by arrows, dots or the arrow keys, and nothing auto-advances
  • Trainer cards that open in place with a real line about each person, not a restated job title
  • App stepper you click through, one step readable at a time
  • A reading progress bar, and a nav bar that takes the colour of the chapter you are in

On colour

  • Every accent is desaturated and dark — rich rather than bright, and all clear contrast on the porcelain ground
  • They sit at about the same value, so no chapter shouts over its neighbours
  • Red is not one of them. It is spent only on things you can click, so red keeps meaning “action”
  • All of the motion switches off for a visitor who has asked for reduced motion
